## 3. Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

1. Communicate respectfully and professionally.
2. Stay on topic and keep discussions relevant to the repository.
3. Provide clear technical details when reporting issues or proposing changes.
4. Respect intellectual property, license restrictions, and trademark notices.
5. Disclose conflicts of interest when relevant.
6. Avoid sharing secrets, credentials, private data, customer data, or confidential materials.
7. Follow `LICENSE.md`, `NOTICE`, `SUPPORT.md`, and `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`.

## 4. Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not allowed:

1. Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption.
2. Hate speech, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, or abusive language.
3. Sexualized content, unwanted attention, or inappropriate conduct.
4. Doxxing, publishing private information, or exposing credentials/secrets.
5. Spam, scams, phishing, malware, deceptive links, or social engineering.
6. Attempts to evade license restrictions, remove legal notices, or encourage unauthorized commercial use.
7. Misrepresenting affiliation, endorsement, certification, ownership, or commercial authorization.
8. Using WHMCS or any third-party trademark in a misleading or infringing manner.
9. Repeated off-topic demands, commercial solicitations, or pressure for unpaid commercial support.

## 5. Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. Editing, hiding, or deleting comments.
2. Closing issues, discussions, or pull requests.
3. Requesting clarification or behavior changes.
4. Temporarily or permanently blocking participants.
5. Reporting abuse to the platform provider or relevant authorities.
6. Refusing support, review, collaboration, or commercial negotiation.
7. Preserving evidence for legal or compliance purposes.

Enforcement decisions are made at the maintainers' discretion to protect the project, its contributors, its rights holder, and third-party rights holders.

## 6. Reporting

If you observe unacceptable behavior, report it through the repository's available reporting mechanism or contact the maintainers through an approved private channel if one is provided. Do not publish sensitive personal data, credentials, exploit details, or confidential information in public reports.

## 7. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when a participant represents the project or interacts with others about the project. It does not grant any license rights, commercial rights, trademark rights, support entitlement, or partnership status.

## 8. Relationship to Legal Documents

This Code of Conduct supplements but does not replace `LICENSE.md`, `NOTICE`, `SUPPORT.md`, or `COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`. If conduct also violates legal or license terms, the maintainers and rights holder may pursue license enforcement and legal remedies in addition to community moderation.
