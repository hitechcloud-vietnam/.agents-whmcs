# WHMCS Ticket Survey Workflow

## Purpose
Step-by-step guide for triggering and managing ticket surveys in WHMCS.

## Prerequisites
- Survey configured
- Ticket closed
- Email system active
- Survey triggers set

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Survey Trigger
- Ticket closed
- Trigger criteria met
- Survey configuration
- Prepare survey

### Step 2: Survey Selection
- Select survey type:
  - CSAT (1-5 scale)
  - NPS (0-10)
  - Custom survey
  - Multi-question
- Configure survey

### Step 3: Recipient Selection
- Identify survey recipient
- Verify email address
- Check permissions
- Prepare personalization

### Step 4: Survey Customization
- Personalize message
- Add ticket details
- Include context
- Set tone

### Step 5: Delivery
- Send survey email
- Include survey link
- Set response period
- Configure reminders

### Step 6: Response Tracking
- Monitor survey opens
- Track responses
- Collect answers
- Record completion

### Step 7: Data Collection
- Gather ratings
- Collect comments
- Aggregate scores
- Store results

### Step 8: Analysis
- Analyze results
- Calculate scores
- Identify trends
- Generate insights

### Step 9: Action Planning
- Plan improvements
- Address issues
- Share results
- Update processes

### Step 10: Reporting
- Generate survey reports
- Track trends
- Measure effectiveness
- Update metrics

## Survey Best Practices
- Keep it short
- Use clear questions
- Make it mobile-friendly
- Follow up on responses

## Verification Checklist
- [ ] Survey configured
- [ ] Sent
- [ ] Responses collected
- [ ] Analyzed
- [ ] Reported
