# WHMCS Quarterly Review Workflow

## Purpose
Complete quarterly business review

## Prerequisites
- WHMCS installed
- Admin access
- Reporting access

## Step 1: Generate Quarterly Revenue Report

Navigate to: Reports > Revenue

Generate Q[X] report:
- Total revenue
- Recurring revenue
- New revenue
- Lost revenue

## Step 2: Review Client Metrics

Navigate to: Reports > Clients > New Signups

Review:
- New clients
- Churned clients
- Retention rate
- Client value

## Step 3: Analyze Product Performance

Navigate to: Reports > Products

Review:
- Top selling products
- Underperforming products
- Average order value
- Product margins

## Step 4: Review Support Metrics

Navigate to: Reports > Support

Check:
- Ticket volume
- Average response time
- Resolution time
- CSAT scores

## Step 5: Financial Reconciliation

Navigate to: Billing > Invoices

Review:
- Outstanding invoices
- Overdue payments
- Refunds issued
- Failed payments

## Step 6: Review Expenses

Document quarterly expenses:
- Hosting costs
- License fees
- Payment processing fees
- Support costs

## Step 7: Analyze Trends

Compare to previous quarter:
- Revenue growth
- Client growth
- Churn rate changes
- Support trends

## Step 8: Review Goals Progress

Compare actual vs. targets:
- Revenue targets
- Client acquisition
- Cost control
- Customer satisfaction

## Step 9: Competitive Analysis

Review:
- Market pricing
- Competitor offerings
- Value proposition
- Differentiation

## Step 10: Create Action Items

Document for next quarter:
- Revenue opportunities
- Cost reduction
- Process improvements
- Product changes

## Quarterly Review Checklist

- [ ] Revenue report generated
- [ ] Client metrics reviewed
- [ ] Product performance analyzed
- [ ] Support metrics reviewed
- [ ] Financial reconciliation done
- [ ] Expenses reviewed
- [ ] Trends analyzed
- [ ] Goals progress reviewed
- [ ] Competitive analysis done
- [ ] Action items created
